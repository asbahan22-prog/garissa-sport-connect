Garissa SportsConnect

Connecting talent. Empowering athletes. Transforming sports.

The problem

Players, fixtures, and trials in Garissa are scattered across notebooks and chats. A good athlete can be invisible to a coach one sub-county away, and an under-18 player has no consistent privacy boundary.

Who it is for

  • Athletes building a record and applying for trials
  • Coaches running squads, training, and attendance
  • Scouts searching and shortlisting talent
  • Club admins registering organizations and teams
  • Organizers publishing fixtures and results
  • A super admin verifying clubs and moderating the platform

What the first release does

Accounts and role dashboards, athlete profiles, clubs and teams, a county league with automatic standings, discovery, opportunities and applications, performance charts, notifications, and an admin queue. Records persist in MongoDB.

How it is built

React and Tailwind on the front. Node, Express, and MongoDB on the back. JWT and bcrypt protect accounts. Recharts draws the tables and charts. Cloudinary is optional for photos. Render and Vercel are the hosting targets.

Safeguarding

Athletes under 18 need guardian consent. They stay out of the public directory. Scouts do not see their phone, photos, or measurements. Coaches of that squad can see guardian contact. Accounts can be reported and suspended.
